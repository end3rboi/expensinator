How it works
1Fill in the shoot details once

They go at the top of every sheet. Pick the period with the From and To dates.

2Pick a sheet and add expenses

Date, what it was, amount (up to 2 decimals). The date stays set so you can add a whole day quickly. You can type 200+150 in amount.

3Tap an expense to fix it

Change the date, name, amount or sheet, or delete it.

4Check the Summary tab

It shows the summary sheet as it will print, a day-by-day breakdown, and every sheet in the file.

5Export

Excel to keep editing, PDF to print or send. Both have the summary sheet first, then every sheet.

Everything saves as you type and stays after you close the browser. On iPhone, use Share → Add to Home Screen so Safari never clears it.
